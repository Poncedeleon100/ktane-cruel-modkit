On the Subject of Colorful Instructions

“These bomb-creators must be hard-wired at making d-LED-ful modules!” -Guy who made the Morsematics flavor text

Note: If your module’s components aren’t exactly [Wires], you are looking at the wrong section of this manual!

  • To solve the module, cut every wire. Each LED provides instructions on how to cut the wires.
  • Begin by cutting any wire, except a wire with a black LED. Upon cutting a wire, follow the instruction of the LED below it. The first LED used will be this wire’s LED.
  • If an LED has a white star, use the underlined word in the parenthesis; otherwise, use the bolded text in the parentheses.
  • If the LED does not have a star, give it a white star if there is an even amount of batteries; otherwise, give it a black star.
  • Unless otherwise stated, all wires wrap around and each rule excludes its own wire.
Red Cut the wire to the (right/left) of this wire.
Orange Cut any wire with an LED that matches the color of the LED to the (left/right), including that wire.
Yellow Cut any wire with a (black/white) star.
Lime Cut any wire to the (left/right) of this wire. Do not wrap around unless this is the (left/right)-most wire.
Green Cut this wire and refer to the LED to the (right/left) of this wire.
Cyan Cut any wire at an (even/odd) position, including this wire.
Blue Cut any wire with its LED (used/not used).
Purple Cut the wire with the same number as the (first/last) applicable digit in the SN. If it’s already cut, or there are no applicable digits, cut any wire, including this wire.
Pink Cut any wire with the number (1-3/5-7).
Black Do not cut this wire under any circumstance. Only use this rule when there are no other valid wires, giving priority to the leftmost wire. Refer to the LED to the (right/left) of this one.
White Cut any wire with the color of the (last/left-most) LED used.

Pressing the “❖” symbol or incurring a strike will reset the wires but not the solution. Cutting an invalid wire will cause a strike. Press the “❖” symbol if there are no valid wires to cut.
